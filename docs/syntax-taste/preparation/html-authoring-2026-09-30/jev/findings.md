Minor phrasing emphasis varies, as required by the rewriting exercise. We cannot infer the classifier's reasons or attribute the differences to wording rather than inference variability.

The direction disagreement is material. HYBRID investigates ordinary HTML-building component functions plus optional tree layout; UNDECIDED withholds architecture selection. These both permit useful research but do not amount to an architectural consensus. The low concentration of the first two distributions reinforces that limitation.

The evidence-step disagreement also remains: a fragment gate establishes missing expressiveness and diagnostic mechanics, while a broader controlled agent trial estimates editing reliability. Neither substitutes for the other. The current document-only prototype cannot perform matched reusable-tree extraction, and a larger trial of only its existing subset would not prove that capability.

Project judgment: first scope a reusable node/fragment experiment with ordinary typed helpers, executable assertions and contextual/source-map diagnostics; then run broader matched edits once both candidates can perform the same task. Keep the architecture open. This prioritization follows the observed missing mechanism and is **not** a unanimous Jev recommendation.

The uniformly low Noul outputs are advisory classifications consistent with the bounded dataset; they establish no general reliability or correctness theorem. The investigation observed 13 checked/emitted candidates and 52 direct renders: 28 exact markup matches, 22 expected domain failures and 2 blank-publication mutation successes. Attached assertions were checked/emitted but not executed.

Requests, raw responses, response metadata and wording-audit.json are the retained evidence. Root and independent agents performed wording/semantic checks before sending and reconciled the disagreements afterward. Agreement on individual constraints would still require executable validation.
